# IGP24 Roadmap

## Stage 0

Repo setup, `igp24` environment, exact polynomial utilities, smoke tests, JSONL
ledger, and verifier stubs.

## Stage 1

Untargeted generation, `target_r` generation, modular proxy scoring, and more
systematic bounded local search.

## Stage 2

Structured polynomial families, group metadata, better cycle filters, and
PARI/MAGMA wrappers.

## Stage 3

Optional Rust/PyO3 or multiprocessing verifier bridge, batched exact
verification, leaderboard-aware target selection, and long training runs.
